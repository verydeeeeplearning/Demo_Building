# Intent Primitives

- Sense: gather information from light, moisture, distance, button, temperature, or motion.
- Decide: compare, threshold, toggle, time, sequence, or map a value.
- Act: show, light, beep, move, display, or signal.
- Feedback: visual, sound, text, motion, or mixed.
- Constraint: breadboard, Arduino-compatible, low-voltage, beginner-safe, available kit.
- Safety flag: mains, high current, heat, unknown voltage, unsupported actuator.
