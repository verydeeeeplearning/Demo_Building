# Safety Policy

Reject or reframe direct wiring instructions for mains voltage, high-current loads, battery charging, heat/fire projects, unknown power supplies, and relays connected to real AC loads.

Use safe low-voltage substitutions: Arduino USB 5V, LED model, buzzer model, display model, or a non-energized relay concept. Motor and servo requests require explicit current warnings; DC motors require a driver abstraction before any final circuit.

Safety explanations should be brief and age appropriate. Never provide step-by-step dangerous wiring.
